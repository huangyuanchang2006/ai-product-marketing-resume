## Stack

Static website with manual updates for V1. The framework/static-site generator and hosting provider are still undecided.

## Users

- Primary: existing followers and new readers arriving from Xiaohongshu and other content platforms. They should quickly understand Huang Yuanchang's interests, methods, and ongoing development, then discover selected work worth exploring.
- Secondary: recruiters and internship reviewers evaluating education, language ability, experience, and representative projects. Their needs should be served primarily through a dedicated About page without turning the whole site into a conventional résumé portfolio.

## Product Purpose

The site is a personal publishing and curation home for a Waseda SILS first-year student from Hangzhou with Chinese, English, and Japanese ability and cross-platform content-creation experience.

Its central purpose is to present a "builder who is growing systematically": the process of learning, organizing knowledge, creating, and improving is the narrative; professionalism supports that story without becoming the main performance. Success means strengthening the relationship with current and future readers, supporting audience growth, and creating a credible home for future internship or employment opportunities.

## Positioning

The site emphasizes process over a collection of finished outcomes. It shows how scattered inputs become organized thinking and public work, including the methods, experiments, and growth trajectory behind the output. It must remain honest about the creator's current stage and must not frame him as a senior operator or established expert.

## Operating Context

- The homepage introduces the positioning and provides selected entry points into the site's main areas.
- Curated posts are the highest-priority content area. Cross-platform articles are selected and accompanied by editorial context explaining why each was made and what it explores. Candidate themes include Japanese-learning methods, content-creation methodology, and observations of life in Japan.
- The Obsidian Growth Lab is the second-priority area. It publishes one or two curated case studies rather than exposing the full vault. Each case should demonstrate the CODE sequence: Capture, Organize, Distill, Express.
- Video is included inside the curated-content area as a small showcase in V1, not as a standalone top-level section.
- The About page contains education, language ability, work experience, and representative projects for professional readers.
- V1 content is curated and exported manually. Obsidian is treated as a source of selected snapshots rather than a live public database.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Chinese is the primary language.
- A full English version and language switcher are required.
- Japanese-language support is undecided and is not part of the confirmed V1 scope.
- V1 is a static site built once for launch and maintained manually afterward.
- V1 does not include a standalone video section, full or live Obsidian-vault publishing, three complete language versions, comments, user accounts, or other complex dynamic interaction.
- Potential Notion-to-Obsidian automation is deferred until V2 or later; the existing `wj0s3ph/obsidian-notion-plugin` pipeline may support that future work.
- The exact posts, videos, and Obsidian case-study topics included at launch are undecided.

## Product Principles

1. Show the path, not just the result: reveal the thinking and transformation behind selected work.
2. Curate instead of mirroring: publish intentional selections with context rather than copying entire feeds or knowledge bases.
3. Earn credibility through specificity: use real projects, methods, constraints, and evidence while staying honest about the creator's current stage.
4. Keep the creator-reader relationship primary: professional evaluation should be supported without flattening the site into a résumé.
5. Start focused and maintainable: V1 should be a deliberate bilingual static site whose content can be updated by hand.
